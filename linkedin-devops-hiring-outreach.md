## Daily Outreach Checklist

- Search 3-5 keyword combinations.
- Open only relevant profiles or job posts.
- Verify the person is actually hiring, recruiting, or connected to a target company.
- Send 10-20 high-quality connection requests, not mass requests.
- Track each send below.
- Follow up only after they accept or after a role-specific conversation starts.

- Avoid sending to unrelated profiles.

- Stop if LinkedIn shows warnings, captcha, or send limits.
- Keep outreach professional and concise.
