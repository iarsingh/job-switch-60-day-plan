# Job Search System

## Weekly Application Target

- 30 targeted applications
- 30 referral requests
- 15 recruiter messages
- 5 hiring manager messages
- 3 LinkedIn posts or project updates

## Best Search Keywords

Use combinations of:

- DevOps Engineer Kubernetes GCP
- Senior DevOps Engineer Terraform Kubernetes
- MLOps Engineer GCP Kubernetes
- Platform Engineer Kubernetes Terraform
- Cloud Infrastructure Engineer GCP
- AI Infrastructure Engineer Kubernetes
- SRE Kubernetes Observability
- ML Platform Engineer

## Application Rules

Apply when you match at least 60-70 percent of the role.

Prioritize jobs with:

- Kubernetes
- Terraform
- GCP or AWS
- CI/CD
- Python or Go
- Observability
- MLOps, ML platform, or AI infra

Avoid spending time on:

- unclear job descriptions
- unpaid assignments before screening
- roles far below your experience
- roles requiring deep frontend if your target is infra

## Daily Workflow

1. Search jobs for 20 minutes.
2. Save 8-10 relevant roles.
3. Customize resume headline and top skills for 2-3 roles.
4. Apply to 2-4 strong matches.
5. Message employees for referrals.
6. Track status in `application-tracker.md`.
7. Follow up after 4-5 business days.

## Referral Strategy

Best people to message:

- engineers in the same team
- engineering managers
- recruiters
- alumni or ex-colleagues
- people posting about hiring

Message should be short:

- who you are
- why the role fits
- 2-3 proof points
- direct ask for referral or guidance
